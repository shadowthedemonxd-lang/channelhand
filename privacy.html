Privacy Policy

Effective November 1, 2026

Channelhand runs on your own computer. We do not operate servers that receive, store or process your YouTube data.

This policy explains what Channelhand (“Channelhand”, “we”) does with information when you use the Channelhand tool and this website. Channelhand is a local tool that an AI assistant can call to upload, edit, list and measure videos on YouTube channels that you authorize.

1. YouTube API Services

Channelhand uses YouTube API Services to do its work. By using Channelhand you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Information obtained through YouTube API Services is also subject to the Google Privacy Policy.

2. Data we access

When you connect a channel through Google's sign-in page and grant permission, Channelhand can access:

  • Channel information: your channel ID and uploads list, to know which channel to act on.
  • Video information: titles, descriptions, tags, category, privacy status and public statistics (views, likes, comments) of videos on your channel.
  • Channel analytics: views, watch time, average view duration and subscribers gained or lost, through the YouTube Analytics API (read-only).
  • Files you choose: the video and thumbnail files on your computer that you ask Channelhand to upload.
  • An authorization token issued by Google, which lets Channelhand act on your channel without your password.

Channelhand never receives or stores your Google password.

3. How the data is used

Channelhand uses this data only to carry out the action you ask for — uploading a video, changing its details, listing your videos or reporting your analytics — and to show you the result in your AI assistant. It does not use the data for advertising, profiling, or to train any model, and it does not combine it with data from other sources.

Your AI assistant receives the results of the actions you request (for example a video link or a view count) so it can answer you. That assistant is provided by a separate company under its own privacy policy.

4. Storage

The authorization token is stored in a file on your own computer and is refreshed automatically by Google's libraries. Video files stay where you keep them. Channelhand keeps no copies of your videos, metadata or analytics, and nothing is stored on any server we control.

5. Sharing

Channelhand sends your data only to Google (YouTube API Services) in order to perform the action you requested. We do not sell, rent or share your data with anyone else.

6. Cookies and device data

This website does not use cookies, analytics, advertising or tracking scripts. The Channelhand tool does not store, access or collect information on your device or browser beyond the token file described above. It loads fonts from Google Fonts, which receives standard request information such as your IP address.

7. Revoking access

You can remove Channelhand's access to your Google Account and YouTube channels at any time from the Google security settings page. After you revoke access, the stored token stops working.

8. Deleting your data

Because your data stays on your computer, you control its deletion: delete the token file (token.json) in the Channelhand folder, or uninstall Channelhand. If you contact us and ask, we will help you confirm that no data remains. Any YouTube data Channelhand had cached during a session is not kept after the action ends; we do not keep YouTube data for longer than 30 days.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. Significant changes will be noted at the top of this page.
